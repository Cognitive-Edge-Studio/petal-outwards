# Wellora

A concept platform for individual doctors and clinics. Wellora uses Maya to extend their presence, helping patients prepare for visits, revisit approved explanations, and carry questions into their next conversation with a care professional.

The persona can represent a named doctor or the clinic as an organization, depending on its configuration. The care team defines the knowledge and role behind that presence.

## Care has a conversation between visits

A question arrives after the appointment. A patient wants to prepare for a first visit. Someone remembers part of an explanation but would like to revisit the material their doctor shared.

Wellora gives those conversations a familiar place to continue. Maya can make a doctor’s or clinic’s approved information more available while the people providing care are with other patients.

The concept centers on preparation, understanding, and connection. A patient should feel better equipped to speak with their care team, with a clear route back to the people responsible for their care.

## A doctor’s presence. A clinic’s welcome.

An individual doctor can configure a Maya persona around the educational materials and explanations they choose to share. It reflects their approach to patient communication within a defined role.

A clinic can offer an organizational persona instead. It introduces services, explains the visit experience, answers common preparation questions, and directs requests to the appropriate team. Its presence reflects the clinic’s shared identity and approved knowledge.

Wellora names that identity clearly. A patient can tell whether the AI representative is speaking for a particular doctor or for the clinic. That distinction helps them understand who owns the information and where a request will go.

## Arrive with the questions that matter

Before a visit, Wellora can share the clinic’s approved preparation information and help a patient organize what they want to discuss. A conversation can collect their own account of their concerns and turn it into a summary they can review.

That summary might include the reason for the visit, questions they do not want to forget, and the information they want to bring. It gives the patient a voice in preparing the conversation, rather than asking them to complete the same introduction repeatedly.

Maya is explicit about being an AI representative. It supports preparation and education within its defined role. Clinical assessment, diagnosis, treatment decisions, and changes to a care plan belong to the care professional.

## Keep the explanation close

After a visit, a patient can return to the approved educational material their doctor or clinic has made available. Wellora can help them find a relevant explanation, revisit its language, and collect questions that still need a human answer.

Where the care team has explicitly shared a personal follow-up explanation, the patient can revisit it within their own relationship. The persona does not infer a new care plan from an earlier conversation.

Maya’s continuity helps a returning patient pick up where they left off. Their questions stay within their relationship with that doctor or clinic; one person’s private context is not used to answer another person’s question.

## Make the next human conversation easier

Some questions need the doctor. Others belong with the clinic’s administrative team. Wellora can make that next step clearer and prepare the context for the appropriate person.

A patient can review the question and summary before it is passed on. The experience distinguishes a request that has been prepared from one that a person has reviewed or resolved. That clarity matters when patients are waiting for a response.

The care team can learn from common gaps in its educational material. A recurring question becomes an opportunity for a reviewed explanation, improving what the persona can offer without silently expanding its clinical role.

## A familiar presence around human care

Wellora supplies the patient experience: a welcoming identity, a way to prepare, a place to return to approved explanations, and a path back to the care team. Maya supplies the configured persona and the continuity that connects those moments.

For a doctor, this can extend the availability of their own explanations. For a clinic, it can create a consistent shared welcome while helping people reach the right member of the team.

This concept explores how care professionals could be more present between conversations. The doctor and patient relationship remains the foundation; Wellora helps that relationship continue.
