# Juriva

A concept platform for independent lawyers and law firms. Juriva uses Maya to extend a professional’s presence: helping clients prepare, understand the next step, and keep a conversation moving while their lawyer is occupied.

The persona can represent a named lawyer or a firm, depending on its configuration. The people behind that identity remain responsible for its knowledge, boundaries, and decisions.

## When a question cannot wait for a meeting

A client thinks of a question after a consultation. Someone approaching a firm for the first time wants to understand its services. A lawyer is deep in preparation and cannot keep returning to the same introductory explanations.

Juriva begins with those moments. It gives people a place to ask, prepare, and find the information their lawyer or firm has chosen to make available. The experience feels connected to the practice they already know, rather than starting a new conversation with a disconnected assistant.

The aim is more useful availability: a clearer next step for the client and a better starting point for the professional when they return.

## One lawyer’s voice. A firm’s shared presence.

An independent lawyer can build a Maya persona around their way of explaining services, preparing clients, and setting expectations. It draws from materials they approve and represents that lawyer’s defined role.

A firm can instead offer an organizational persona. It introduces the practice, explains common processes, and helps an inquiry reach the right team. Its knowledge belongs to the firm’s approved collection, rather than being assembled from every lawyer’s private conversations.

Juriva can make those identities clear at the point of contact. A client knows whether they are speaking with the AI representative of a particular lawyer or of the firm. Configuration determines whose presence is extended and what the persona may do.

## A better first conversation

Before a first meeting, the persona can explain the consultation process, share an approved preparation checklist, and help someone organize the questions they want to ask. A prospective client can describe what brought them to the practice without repeatedly retelling the same introduction.

For a firm, Juriva can turn that introduction into a concise inquiry for the appropriate team. For an independent lawyer, it can prepare a useful summary for their own review. The professional decides whether to take the matter forward.

Specific legal judgment, strategy, and commitments stay with the lawyer. If the question needs their assessment, the persona helps the person formulate it and carries the context into a human conversation.

## Continuity between conversations

Once a relationship exists, Juriva can help a client revisit explanations and materials that have been made available to them. They can return to their preparation notes, clarify a process, or gather questions for the next meeting.

Maya’s relationship context supports that continuity. A returning client does not have to start with their introduction every time. Their conversation remains specific to their relationship with the lawyer or firm; it does not become general knowledge for another client.

Juriva distinguishes an approved explanation from a new professional decision. It can help someone understand the next step already communicated, while a new assessment or change in direction returns to the lawyer.

## Prepared for the person who takes over

When a conversation needs a professional, a useful handoff includes the person’s question, what they have already shared, and what remains unresolved. The receiving lawyer can begin with context rather than asking the client to repeat the exchange.

The persona makes that transition visible. It does not pretend that a lawyer has reviewed a message or that the practice has accepted an engagement. Juriva’s role is to prepare the conversation and make the request clear.

The practice can also review recurring questions. If an explanation is repeatedly missing, the responsible people can improve their approved material and publish a reviewed update to the persona.

## More presence, with the same professional ownership

Juriva is the client-facing platform: the practice’s identity, the entry into a relationship, the preparation experience, and the route back to a human professional. Maya powers the persona underneath, bringing approved knowledge and relationship continuity to those interactions.

For an independent lawyer, that can mean a more available expression of their own practice. For a firm, it can mean a coherent shared presence across its teams. In both cases, the professional relationship stays at the center.

This is a concept case study: an exploration of how a practice could extend its availability through Maya, with human expertise guiding what comes next.
